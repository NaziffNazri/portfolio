BOATMU UI/UX CASE STUDY
ROLE: UI/UX DESIGNER FIGMA

The Frictionless Ticketing Platform for Modern Event Creators

Festmu is a comprehensive ticketing and registration platform designed to bridge the gap between complex event registration and diversity of event.

Visit Website

MAIN GOAL

Create a platform for events in Malaysia, moving away from niche specific platforms to a unified ecosystem, also with simple flow registration.

Problems

Complex flow : Users must navigate long, multi-step registration forms. This high friction leads to cart abandonment, where users lose interest.

Manual Work : Many local organizers still use Google Forms, WhatsApp, or manual bank transfers. This forces them to spend hours manually verifying receipts and typing participant data into Excel leaving huge room for human error.

High Entry Barrier : Competitors require organizers to be "tech-savvy" just to list an event, leaving local grassroots events behind.

Solutions

Frictionless Flow : A 3 step checkout process designed for high conversion rates, event with multiple registration quantity.

Removal of Manual Systems : Replace messy spreadsheets and WhatsApp screenshots with a centralized, automated database and reports.

Assisted Registration: The "Festmu Team" acts as a bridge. The organizer provides the event details via a simple brief, and our team handles the technical setup.

07. SUMMARY

FINAL NOTES

Completing Boatmu marks a significant milestone in my journey as a designer. As my first end-to-end UI/UX project, it served as a high-stakes training ground where I transitioned from theoretical knowledge to delivering a live, functional product for a startup.

KEY TAKE AWAY

Learning Experience:

Handling the entire lifecycle from initial UX research and user flow mapping to high-fidelity prototype taught me the importance of design consistency and how every small interaction impacts the overall user journey.

Problem-Solving Under Pressure:

Building for a startup meant finding the "sweet spot" between user needs and business feasibility. I learned to prioritize features that reduced friction, such as a simplified the available date for trip selection logic.

Foundation for Growth:

This project provided me with the hands-on experience and confidence needed to tackle complex product design challenges in the future.
