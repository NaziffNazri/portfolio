BOATMU UI/UX Case Study Startup Boat Booking Platform
BOATMU UI/UX CASE STUDY
ROLE: UI/UX DESIGNER FIGMA

Seamless Boat Booking Experience for Travel Enthusiasts

Boatmu is an online boat ticket booking platform designed to streamline the process of finding, comparing, and booking boat trips across various island destinations with zero friction.

Visit Website

MAIN GOAL

To provide users a intuitive way to find the the best marine trip, in a seamless booking experience.

Problems

Fragmentation: Information is scattered across Facebook groups, Instagram DMs, and WhatsApp. Users have to "hunt" for prices.

The Payment Loop: Users must send a bank-in slip manually via WhatsApp and wait for a human to verify it. This creates "booking anxiety" Did they get my money? Is this a scam?

Lack of Visibility: Operators struggle to showcase their services and to stand out in the competitive market.

Manual Management: Operators spend 60% of their day answering the same questions ("How much for 3 pax?", "Is there a boat at 9 AM?") instead of managing their fleet.

Solutions

Centralized Discovery: A unified "Island Portal" where users can compare all trip type, destination, price in a single platform. And to reduce the search time in social media.

Automated Trust: Integration of local payment gateways (FPX/Card) with an Instant Confirmation Screen. No more sending screenshots of bank slips.

Clarity: Product implementation on the Boatmu main page, and also create a special link for each provider to showcase all of their trips.

Digitalization: Implemented a trip card that provide all of the information about the trip that breaks down the itinerary, destinations, item include and map.

Sarah, 27

professional Kuala Lumpur 4 Trips / Year
Pain Points:
Hates hidden costs, fears getting scammed by unofficial agents, and finds bank-in slips annoying to track.
Motivation:

Efficiency, transparent pricing, and Instagrammability.

  • Book a snorkeling trip for 4 friends in under 5 minutes.
  • See which boat has a "Sunset Cruise" vs just a "Day Transfer."
  • Trust: Receive an instant confrimation so she knows her seat is 100% secured.

06. PROTOTYPE

HOME PAGE

A user-friendly homepage prioritizes the user's needs on clarity, speed, and "frictionless" interaction.

Information Architecture:

Structured visual hierarchy and distinct section for filters and products.

Encourage Engagement:

Filter system to act as a discovery tool, educating users on the variety of tour experiences and destination available in Boatmu.

Centralized Control:

Compile all the trips and destination into a single, dedicated page, simplifying navigation.

07. SUMMARY

FINAL NOTES

Completing Boatmu marks a significant milestone in my journey as a designer. As my first end-to-end UI/UX project, it served as a high-stakes training ground where I transitioned from theoretical knowledge to delivering a live, functional product for a startup.

KEY TAKE AWAY

Learning Experience:

Handling the entire lifecycle from initial UX research and user flow mapping to high-fidelity prototype taught me the importance of design consistency and how every small interaction impacts the overall user journey.

Problem-Solving Under Pressure:

Building for a startup meant finding the "sweet spot" between user needs and business feasibility. I learned to prioritize features that reduced friction, such as a simplified the available date for trip selection logic.

Foundation for Growth:

This project provided me with the hands-on experience and confidence needed to tackle complex product design challenges in the future.
