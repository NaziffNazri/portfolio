01 / 08 Overview & Objectives
Navigations

Seamless Boat Booking Experience for Travel Enthusiasts

Boatmu is an online boat ticket booking platform designed to streamline the process of finding, comparing, and booking boat trips across various island destinations with zero friction.

Visit Website

MAIN GOAL

To digitize and streamline Malaysia’s marine tourism landscape by transitioning traditional boat operators to a centralized digital management system while giving travelers a seamless, reliable platform to discover and book island hopping, ferry transfers, private charters, and sea activities.

Problems

Manual Operations: Boat operators rely on paper logs, phone calls, WhatsApp messages, and cash payments, leading to double bookings and lost revenue.

Lack of Visibility: Operators struggle to showcase their services and to stand out in the competitive market.

Fragmentation: Users have to "hunt" for prices in social media, as there is no central system dedicated specifically to marine tourism.

The Payment Loop: Users must send a bank-in slip manually via WhatsApp and wait for a human to verify it. This creates "booking anxiety" Did they get my money? Is this a scam?

Solutions

Digitalize System: A single digital dashboard for boat operators to manage booking, seat availability, passenger manifests, and payments in real time.

Clarity: Product implementation on the Boatmu main page, and also create a special link for each provider to showcase all of their trips.

Centrelize: Implemented a trip card that provide all of the information about the trip that breaks down the itinerary, destinations, item include and map.

Automated Trust: Integration of local payment gateways (FPX/Card) with an Instant Confirmation Screen. No more sending screenshots of bank slips.

1. Boat Provider Insights (Operator Side)

Tide & Monsoon Weather Uncertainty

Issue: Schedules frequently shift due to low river tides or sudden sea condition shifts.

Current Friction: Operators manually inform dozens of travelers individually via WhatsApp/phone when trips delay, causing jetty chaos.

Fragmented Direct & Resort Bookings

Issue: Operators balance walk-ins, resort package tie-ins, travel agencies, and direct WhatsApp chats.

Current Friction: Overbooking and double-booked seats occur frequently during school holidays and long weekends.

Manual Manifests & Compliance

Issue: Marine authorities require accurate passenger lists (IC/passport numbers, names) prior to departure.

Current Friction: Handwritten paper manifests at counters create long lines, delay departures, and risk compliance fines.

Payment Reconciliation & Cash Dependency

Issue: Walk-in passengers pay cash or individual DuitNow QR transfers on-site.

Current Friction: Captains lose track of who paid online vs dock, creating major end-of-day accounting headaches.

Key Traveler Pain Points

  • Schedule & Gate Confusion: Uncertainty over departure times, platform locations, and physical ticket passes.
  • Hidden Marine Park Fees: Unclear separation between Marine Park entrance fees, terminal fees, and boat fares.
  • Lack of On-Demand Activities: Snorkeling, turtle trips, and water taxis are hard to book without middleman markup.

3. Recommended Core Features (Field Feedback Driven)

OPERATOR SIDE

Merchant App / Admin Dashboard

Dynamic Schedule & Tide Management

Delay, cancel, or re-route departures with 1-tap, triggering automated SMS/WhatsApp alerts to all booked passengers.

Digital Manifest Generator

Auto-collect IC/passport numbers at checkout and instantly export compliant PDF manifests for marine authorities.

Unified Inventory Sync

Real-time seat allocation across direct sales, resort partners, and walk-in counters to completely eliminate overbooking.

QR Boarding Scanner

Mobile scanner app for boat captains or jetty staff to scan digital QR tickets for frictionless check-in.

TRAVELER SIDE

User App / Web Portal

All-in-One Checkout

Pay boat fare, Marine Park fee, and jetty access fees together in a single transparent transaction.

Live Boat Tracker & Gate Info

Real-time boat status updates, designated jetty counter/platform numbers, and weather advisories.

Island Activity & Water Taxi Hub

Instant booking for local island-hopping, private charters, and snorkeling trips with verified local boatmen.

Offline E-Pass Access

Digital tickets saved offline with embedded QR codes—essential for low-coverage island jetty environments.

04. EMPATHIZE & DEFINE

USER PERSONA

Sarah, 27

Professional Kuala Lumpur 4 Trips / Year
Personality
Spontaneous Tech-Savvy Detail-Oriented Social
User Quote
"I want a seamless, instant way to book island trips with my friends without worrying about scam agents, hidden fees, or sending manual bank-in slips."
Bio

Sarah is a 27-year-old Kuala Lumpur professional who frequently organizes weekend island trips for her friends. Tech-savvy and safety-conscious, she relies on digital tools to plan itineraries but gets frustrated by manual booking steps, hidden costs, and unverified boat operators.

Goals
  • Book group trips (e.g., snorkeling for 4 friends) in under 5 minutes without friction.
  • Easily distinguish between different service packages (e.g., "Sunset Cruise" vs. standard "Day Transfer").
  • Secure instant booking confirmation to guarantee seat availability before traveling.
  • Have a single, trusted platform for managing marine tourism activities across Malaysian islands.
Core Needs
  • Transparent All-in-One Pricing: Upfront breakdown of boat tickets, marine park fees, and add-ons with zero hidden charges.
  • Instant Digital Verification: Real-time seat allocation, direct e-tickets, and instant digital payment confirmation (FPX / e-Wallet).
  • Curated & Visual Listings: High-quality photos, clear trip itineraries, and verified provider reviews for shareable, "Instagrammable" experiences.
Frustrations (Pain Points)
• Manual Payment Workflows: Annoying manual bank-in slips and tracking payment receipts over WhatsApp.
• Fear of Unofficial Agents: Threat of getting scammed by unlicensed middlemen or unofficial booking handles.
• Price Ambiguity: Hidden fees popping up at jetty counters unexpectedly.
• Uncertainty at Departure: Lack of real-time updates regarding weather delays, boat schedules, or jetty boarding platforms.

Completing Boatmu marks a significant milestone in my journey as a designer. As my first end-to-end UI/UX project, it served as a high-stakes training ground where I transitioned from theoretical knowledge to delivering a live, functional product for a startup.

KEY TAKE AWAY

Learning Experience:

Handling the entire lifecycle from initial UX research and user flow mapping to high-fidelity prototype taught me the importance of design consistency and how every small interaction impacts the overall user journey.

Problem-Solving Under Pressure:

Building for a startup meant finding the "sweet spot" between user needs and business feasibility. I learned to prioritize features that reduced friction, such as a simplified the available date for trip selection logic.

Foundation for Growth:

This project provided me with the hands-on experience and confidence needed to tackle complex product design challenges in the future.
